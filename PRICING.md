# Plans and paid features

> **Paid plans are not open yet.** Noetic is free while it is in early access. The prices below are what we plan to
> charge; they may change before plans open, and everyone with an account will be told first.

## At a glance

| | Free | Pro | Overdrive |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | $0 | **$20** for 30 days | **$60** for 30 days |
| Requests a day | 10 | 400 | 2,000 |
| The queue | A short wait on every request | None | None |
| Workers on a build | Standard | More | Most |
| Included credit | none | none | $6 |
| API calls | Count as requests | Count as requests | Count as requests |

A plan is a pass for thirty days. It does not renew by itself.

The person who buys the seats invites the others with a code. Enterprise covers things a price list cannot: your own
limits, invoicing, a named contact. Write to support@snns.dev.

## Pay as you go

Credit you buy once and use when you need it, with no plan.

| Pack | Price | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $5 | $5 |
| Builder | $20 | $21 |
| Studio | $50 | $54 |

Credit is used by how much work a request takes: a quick answer costs a fraction of a cent; a full build, which is
many steps of planning, building, testing and review, typically costs well under a dollar. Your balance and what each
request used are shown in the app.

## What paying changes

- **No queue.** Free requests wait in a short line; paid ones start at once.
- **More workers.** Builds run more of their steps at the same time, and bring in stronger reviewers.
- **Higher daily limits**, and credit for the heavy jobs.
- **The API** at real volume: each call is one request on your plan.

## Ways to pay

Cards, PayPal and several cryptocurrencies. Card payments are handled by a payment company acting as the seller;
Noetic never sees or stores a card number.

## Refunds

Unused plans and credit are refunded where the law requires it, or where we plainly failed to deliver what was
bought. Credit that has been used is not refunded. Write to support@snns.dev.
