# Access

## Why there is a door

Noetic Suite runs real work for each request: planning, building, running, reviewing. That costs capacity, and the
product is new. Letting people in a few at a time is how it stays fast and how the people inside get answers when
something breaks.

## Who we say yes to

- Small developers, solo makers, students building something real.
- People who need the tool for work they are trying to finish and can say what that work is.
- People who will test it and report what they find.

## Who we say no to, for now

- Applications with no stated use.
- Resellers, bulk or automated sign-ups, and accounts shared between many people.
- Anyone whose stated use is something the [acceptable-use rules](LEGAL.md#2-acceptable-use) forbid.

## How an application is judged

A person reads it. They look for a real person, a real use, and a way to write back. There is no score and no
waiting-list number. If the answer is yes you get an invitation by email; if you hear nothing in two weeks, the answer
was no for now, and you are welcome to apply again later with more detail.

## What an account can do

- Use Chat, Cowork, Code and Create within its plan's daily limit.
- Keep projects, download them, and delete them.
- Download the Windows app.
- Write to support from inside Noetic.

## What an account cannot do

- Run commands, plugins or connected services on our servers.
- See how the engine is built, or which services stand behind it.
- Share its sign-in. An account is for one person.

## Joining the team

Sometimes we invite people who use Noetic well to help: in support, in development, or in testing. That invitation
arrives inside Noetic, from the owner, in the owner's own words. Nobody from S&NS will ask you for your password, a
sign-in code or payment details to "join the team". If someone does, tell support.

## Losing access

Accounts are suspended for breaking the acceptable-use rules, for attacking the service, or for sharing the account
or the desktop app. You can ask for a review at support@snns.dev.
