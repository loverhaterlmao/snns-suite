# Support

| You want to… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Ask about your account or how something works | Inside Noetic: **user menu → Contact support**. You see the answer in the same place. |
| Write without an account | The form at [noetic.lol/support](https://noetic.lol/support), or email **support@snns.dev** |
| Report something broken | Same places; choose *Something is broken*. Say what you did, what you expected, what happened |
| Report a security weakness | See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Not in a public issue |
| Send a legal or privacy notice | The form, topic *A legal or privacy matter*, or **support@snns.dev** with "Legal" in the subject |
| Fix a mistake in these guides | Open an issue in this repository |

## What happens to your message

It becomes a case. One person on the team takes it; once taken, their colleagues cannot open it, so what you write is
read by the person helping you and by those who oversee the team, and by nobody else. Legal messages go to the owner
alone.

## What support will never ask for

Your password, a sign-in or device code, a recovery code, card details, or payment to anyone's personal account. If
someone claiming to be S&NS asks for any of these, do not answer, and tell us.

## Response times

We are a small team. Most messages are answered within two working days. If the service is down, that is the first
thing we are already looking at.
