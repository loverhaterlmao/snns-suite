# Questions people ask

### What is Noetic Suite?

One assistant that does the whole job. You ask in a sentence; it plans the work, builds it, runs it, reviews it,
fixes what it finds and hands you the result, with a plain report of what was and was not done.

### How do I get in?

Apply on the front page of [noetic.lol](https://noetic.lol). A person reads every application. We are taking on
small developers, people who genuinely need the tool, and people willing to test it and tell us what broke.

### What does it cost?

Nothing during early access, within a daily limit. Planned prices are in [PRICING.md](PRICING.md).

### Is there an API?

Yes: [API.md](API.md). Make a key under *Sign-in and security* and point your tool at `https://noetic.lol/v1`.

### Is there a desktop app?

For Windows, yes: signed-in accounts get it at `noetic.lol/download`. It is not code-signed yet, so Windows warns
about an unknown publisher; check the file against the SHA-256 shown on the download page.

### And on a phone?

Noetic works in a phone's browser, and can be added to the home screen from the browser's menu, where it opens like
an app. A store app is on the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md).

### Who can see what I make?

During early access, accounts on the hosted service share one workspace, so do not put anything into it that other
invited members must not see. Private workspaces for each person are the next big piece of work. The Windows app
keeps its data on your own computer.

### Do you use my work to train anything?

No. We count how Noetic is used (how often, what kind of work), never what you write, and you can switch even that
off under *Sign-in and security*.

### What will it refuse?

A short fixed list that no setting changes: sexual content involving anyone under 18, sexual or nude pictures of
real people, weapons capable of mass casualties, attacks on systems you do not own, and harassment.

### Something broke. Where do I say so?

Inside Noetic: **user menu → Contact support**. Or open an issue in the product's repository. Say what you did, what
you expected and what happened.

### I found a security problem.

Please do not post it publicly. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

### Can I see the source?

No. The products are closed source. These repositories hold the guides and are where problems are reported.

### How do I delete my account?

Write to support@snns.dev from the account's address. Deleting is permanent.
