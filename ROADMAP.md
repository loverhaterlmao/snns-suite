# Roadmap

What we are working toward, in rough order. No dates: things ship when they are right.

## Now

- **Private workspaces.** Each account's chats and projects visible only to that account.
- **Paid plans open.** Pro, Overdrive, Team and Organization seats, pay-as-you-go credit.
- **Mail that flows.** Answers from support arriving by email, not only in the app.

## Next

- **One sign-in for every S&NS project.** Sign up once, on whichever site you are on, and be signed in to all of them.
- **Each product's plans on its own site.**
- **A signed Windows build**, so Windows stops warning about the publisher.
- **API: tool calling and builds.**

## Later

- **Android and iOS apps.**
- **Desktop apps for the other S&NS products.**
- **Connections between the products**, so Noetic can work in your Claw workspace or act on your Discord server when you ask it to.

Want something that is not here? Open an issue and say what you are trying to get done.
