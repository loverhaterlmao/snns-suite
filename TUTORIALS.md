# Noetic Suite: the first hour

Everything here happens at [noetic.lol](https://noetic.lol) after you have signed in, or in the Windows app.

## 1. Ask for something

Noetic has three spaces, chosen at the top of the conversation:

- **Chat**: think something through, get an answer with its sources when it looked things up.
- **Cowork**: hand over a task ("turn these notes into a one-page brief") and get the finished thing back.
- **Code**: build and change software. The conversation stays in front; files, changes and a preview sit beside it.

You do not need a long prompt. One sentence is enough: *"Build me a small arcade game where you catch falling stars."*

## 2. Watch it work, then check the result

For a build, Noetic plans the work, makes it, runs it, reviews it, attacks it for weaknesses, fixes what it finds, and
only then reports. The report says plainly what was built, what was checked, and what was **not** done. If something
could not be built, it says so rather than hiding it.

- The **task list** under the message shows where it is.
- **Preview** opens the result. Games and sites run in a separate, sandboxed frame.
- **Changes** shows every file it wrote. You can restore an earlier point.

## 3. Change it

Say what you want different: *"make the stars fall faster after 30 seconds"*, *"add a settings screen"*. Noetic changes
the project it already built rather than starting over. Paste an error or your own code if you have it; that helps.

## 4. Pictures

Open **Create**. Describe the picture, pick a style, choose a shape. The editor can crop, remove a background, add a
sticker border and export. Two limits are fixed in code and no setting moves them: nothing sexual involving anyone
under 18, and no sexual or nude pictures of real people.

## 5. Projects

**Projects** keeps the things you build. A project has its files, its instructions for Noetic, and its conversations.
Download a project as a zip from its page.

## 6. Keep your account safe

**User menu → Sign-in and security**:

- **Authenticator app**: after your password you are asked for a six-digit code. Save the recovery codes it shows you.
- **Passkeys**: sign in with this device's fingerprint, face or PIN.
- **Usage counts**: Noetic counts how it is used (how often, what kind of work), never what you write. Switch it off
  here if you prefer.

## 7. The free plan, and its limits

A free account has a daily number of requests, and each waits in a short line. That is deliberate: free requests run
on shared capacity. Paid plans, when they open, skip the line and put more workers on every job.

## 8. When something breaks

**User menu → Contact support**. Say what you did, what you expected and what happened. A person on the team takes
your message, and you see their answer in the same place. For a weakness in security, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## 9. The Windows app

See the README for the download. The app keeps its data in a folder beside itself, runs the same Noetic, and needs no
installer. It is the same product, not a second one: what you learn here applies there.

## Giving feedback that helps

We read all of it. The most useful reports have: the request you made, what came back, what you expected instead,
and whether it happens every time. "The game had no pause button" is worth more to us than "it was bad".
