# Changes

What changed, newest first. Dates are the day a change went live.

## 11 November 2026

- **API.** Accounts can make keys and call Noetic from their own programs at `/v1`, in the chat format most tools already speak.
- **Faster builds.** More of a build's workers now run at the same time.
- **Sign-in.** Authenticator apps and passkeys. Accounts can be bound to their own devices.
- **Support.** A contact page, support conversations inside the app, and a desk behind them where each message is taken by one person.
- **Mail.** A mail view for people with a company mailbox.
- **Desktop.** The Windows app can be downloaded by signed-in accounts.
- **Search.** A sitemap and richer page descriptions.

## 10 November 2026

- Noetic Suite opens at noetic.lol, by invitation.
- Defences against scanning and floods, shared with the other S&NS sites.
- Secrets and messages encrypted at rest.
